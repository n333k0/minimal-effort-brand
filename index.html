01 — Brand

A different kind of front row.

Minimal Effort is a cultural events label that curates music, spaces and experiences guided by taste, timing and restraint. Recurring nights include THE MAZE, plus co-produced nights like Second Wind; this system carries all of them.

What we believeThe line every piece of work answers to

We believe cultural impact comes from discernment, not excess.

What the name meansMinimal Effort, read correctly

The meaning

Minimal Effort does not mean low quality, lack of care or disengagement. It means removing everything unnecessary so what matters can speak for itself.

The method

Culture is strongest when it feels natural, unforced and inevitable. Our role is to recognise moments, artists and spaces that are already aligned, and give them the right context to exist.

The position

Always at the forefront of culture, never fighting for attention. The real ones are in the back, away from the sweat and noise.

The voice

Few words, said once. Names, dates, places. No exclamation marks, no hype adjectives, no emoji in headlines.

02 — Logo

Three logos, one alphabet.

07 — Grid, layout & safe zones

Eight compositions.

Every post is built on a 1080 px artboard with a 64 px margin. Pick one composition and keep it for the whole campaign; the logo row always sits on the bottom margin. Logos can also go top left, centre or right.

CompositionsA–H, all live in the builder. Click one to edit it; sizes, margins and spacing are adjustable there.

GridFeed 1080×1350 · margins 64 · 12 columns · 24 gutter — on eight compositions, every kind of wrapping and background

  • Margins 64 px on every side. Letters (not the block edges) sit on the margin: left stacks start exactly on column 1, right stacks end exactly on column 12, the blocks hang into the margin by their padding.
  • Columns 12, gutter 24; text never runs past 10 columns unless it is meant to bleed (Wall, Bands, Mirror).
  • Rhythm stacked lines touch; groups are 44 or 88 apart.
  • Logo row on the bottom margin: centred with centred text, left with left text, right with right text.

08 — Backgrounds & motion

Liquid light.

One family of moving backgrounds in five groups: Chrome (liquid iridescent forms), Prism (spectrum light), Film (analog leaks), Brand light (Lime and Lilac) and Shader (the ME drawn live by WebGL). Every loop is 9–10 s, locked camera, ends on its first frame. Type holds still on top.

Shader labA tool, not a file: the ME (or MINIMAL EFFORT, MNML EFFRT, THE MAZE) drawn live by WebGL in twelve looks — glass, pixel, grit, chrome, holo film, halftone, ripple, echo tunnel, neon, flag wave, glitch, melt. Start from a preset or shuffle, tweak, then export a PNG or record one seamless loop.

Recording runs in real time (10 s at speed 1) and saves an MP4 or WebM at full size. The rendered Shader loops above are also backgrounds in the builder.

Motion rulesHow things move

  • Backgrounds move; type builds once, holds, clears. Six text animations in the builder (blur in, rise, wipe, type, flicker, drift), all on one 10 s loop so posts loop cleanly.
  • Slow and seamless: no zooms, no cuts, no beat-synced flashing.
  • New loops come from a still with Higgsfield (MiniMax H3, 2K, 10 s, start frame = end frame) and must belong to one of the four groups.

11 — Feed

Nine at a time.

Plan the grid in threes: one artist, one type-led post, one moving background per row. Accents alternate by event. Carousels are marked; the first slide is always type-led.

How to plan itRows of three

  • One row, three kinds: an artist photo, a type-led post, a moving background.
  • Alternate the events: THE MAZE (orange, Quzie) never twice in a row; Second Wind and the other nights carry Lime.
  • Carousels open type-led (cover), then image, numbers, quote, end card. One format per carousel.
  • Stories carry the countdown: presale live → tickets live → final release → sold out.
